OUR STORY

WHO We ARE

పెందుర్తి & రాంపురం మిత్రులు • స్నేహం • భక్తి • ఐక్యత

We are MORYA BOYS RAMPURAM, a batch of friends from Rampuram & Pendurthi bound by devotion, brotherhood, and a 10-year tradition. Every Sri Vinayaka Chavithi, we host Lord Ganesha at Salipeta Shuttle Court (Rampura Ramalayam Down) with open hearts, community feasts, and high-energy celebration.
